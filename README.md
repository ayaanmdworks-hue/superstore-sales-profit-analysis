# Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the Superstore dataset to identify patterns in
sales, profitability, customers, products, regions, time periods,
and discounts.

The objective is to transform raw sales data into meaningful
business insights and provide data-driven recommendations.

## Dataset

The dataset contains 9,994 sales transactions with information about:

- Orders
- Customers
- Products
- Categories
- Regions
- Sales
- Quantity
- Discount
- Profit
- Order and shipping dates

## Tools & Technologies

## Analysis Performed

### Data Cleaning
- Checked missing values
- Checked duplicate records
- Converted date columns to datetime
- Investigated unusual sales and profit values

### Sales & Profitability Analysis
- Total sales and total profit
- Overall profit margin
- Sales and profit by category
- Sales and profit by region
- Yearly sales and profit trends
- Monthly sales trends

### Product Analysis
- Top 10 products by sales
- Loss-making products

### Customer Analysis
- Top customers by sales
- Customer segment analysis

### Discount Analysis
- Discount distribution
- Relationship between discount and profit
- Average profit at different discount levels

## Key Insights

- Total sales were approximately $2.30 million.
- Total profit was approximately $286K.
- Overall profit margin was approximately 12.47%.
- Technology generated the highest total sales and profit.
- Furniture had a significantly lower profit margin than the other
  major categories.
- 2017 recorded the highest annual sales.
- November recorded the highest overall monthly sales.
- Higher discounts were generally associated with lower profitability.

## Business Recommendations

- Review pricing, costs, and discount strategies in the Furniture
  category.
- Carefully evaluate high discount levels to protect profitability.
- Prepare inventory and marketing campaigns ahead of high-demand
  periods.
- Investigate products that consistently generate negative profit.
